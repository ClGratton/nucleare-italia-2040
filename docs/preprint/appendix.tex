\section{Audit and error register}\label{app:audit}
\setcounter{table}{0}
The project keeps a register of every error found in its documents, inputs and code since the first version, compiled from the project records on 11 November 2026 \citep{Repo}. It holds 98 entries, each with what was wrong, who found it, the correction, the effect on the nuclear result and the status. By category: wording or claim 33, method 26, cost input 15, source citation 7, data 7, model code 6, document build 4. By status at that date, before the corrections of the present version: fixed 80, declared as limit 12, still open 6. The most frequent kinds of error were claims stronger than the results (23 entries), one-sided simplifications (21) and comparisons of quantities that are not homogeneous (13).

The present version follows an independent audit of 11 November 2026 of the nuclear cost inputs, the other cost inputs, the data and assumptions and the model code, under the rule that every correction applied to one technology must be checked for all the others. Its main findings, all applied here: fuel is not inside the CZK\,407 billion contract, and the capacity to use is the net 976\,MWe per unit; the Vogtle cost was overstated; auction prices implied capital costs below observed ones (about 681\,EUR/kW for ground PV; about 95\,EUR/kWh for batteries of about 6.6 hours, while the regulatory 150.5\,EUR/kWh is for 4 hours, a different product); interest during construction and fixed operating cost were not applied to every technology; exports earned nothing while imports paid the Italian price; the reserve requirement changed with the time step; and two code bugs, a grid threshold that counted new plants only and wind and grid costs that did not follow the rate at 7\%, had both penalised nuclear. A second independent review, of 14 November 2026, led to the bracket-midpoint cost case, to the labels of the two bundles and to the limits stated by direction. A third review, of 15 November 2026, corrected the description of the grid charge and of the critical weeks, labelled the cases with large reactors only and added the condition on the financing of nuclear.
