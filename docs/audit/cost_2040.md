# 2040 overnight capital cost relative to 2024/2025, by technology

Research date: 2026-11-15. Quantity is overnight capex (not LCOE) throughout.
Method note: pages were read through WebFetch, which returns a model-written summary of the page, not raw text. Numbers below were cross-checked only by asking for verbatim quotes; they should be spot-checked against the URLs before publication. NREL ATB pages on atb.nrel.gov were refused (permission), the same pages on atb.nlr.gov (new NREL domain) worked for 2025 ATB. IEA, DNV, IRENA, BNEF gave no readable numbers (see end).
"Ratio" = 2040 / base year. Items marked [interp] are my own interpolation, not a published value.

## Spread by technology

**PV (tracker).** NREL 2025 gives 2040/2024 of 0.41 to 0.64 for Moderate and Advanced (0.85 Conservative). DEA, which is fixed-tilt in a European context with a cost base already about 0.46 M EUR/MWp, is far flatter at 0.83. JRC (2050/2024 0.46-0.57) and Fraunhofer (2045/2024 about 0.65) sit between. Central estimate for 2040: about 0.55 to 0.65 of 2024, with 0.83 as the pessimistic and 0.41 as the aggressive edge. DEA gives no tracker value.

**Onshore wind.** Not found for 2040 in NREL (only 2035). 2035/2023 is 0.88 (Cons), 0.71 (Mod), 0.67 (Adv). DEA gives 0.94 for 2040/2025, much flatter (4.6% learning rate). Wind spread is narrow: 0.7 to 0.95. The NREL ATB base is 2023, not 2024.

**Batteries.** The highest-uncertainty case. NREL 2035/2023 0.78 / 0.55 / 0.34, Fraunhofer utility-scale about 0.61 for 2040 (my interpolation), and DEA cell+pack 0.50 (a component, not the system; the balance-of-system share falls more slowly). Whole-system 2040/2024 plausible range 0.4 to 0.7. I did not find a published 2040 whole-system number in any source I could read.

**Electrolysers.** DEA gives 2040 about 0.35-0.38 of its 2020 values, but the 2020 base is old; relative to 2030 it is 0.77. IEA STEPS 2030/2024 is 0.70. No source gave 2024/2025 and 2040 together. DEA itself says the 2030-2050 projections are "highly uncertain" and the 2040 figure is a mean of 2030 and 2050. Also, for 2024-2025 realised Western projects, the IEA range (2,000-2,600 USD/kW) is far above the DEA 2020 value (1,200-1,300 EUR/kW), so ratios against DEA's 2020 base understate how much of the cost decline is still to come.

**Large nuclear.** Not a smooth learning curve: it depends on the base. Against the first-of-a-kind US series (Vogtle, about 11,000 $/kW, ceiling 15,000 with CPI), projected series 2/3/4 are 0.91 / 0.71 / 0.56 (INL moderate), NOAK 0.42 (MIT), and the optimistic Chinese-like case about 0.40 for the second series. Observed Chinese Sanmen is 0.22 of Vogtle. ATB 2025 is relative to a 2030 reference: 0.96 / 0.82 / 0.57 by 2040. Learning rates used: 8% (ATB), about 10% (MIT, range 8-15%). Korean APR1400 series cost data (Shin Kori 3&4 to Shin Hanul/Saeul, Barakah): not found as a published per-unit overnight series in a source I could read; MIT CANES 2024 explicitly states it does not give APR1400 costs. EIRP/ETI and the MIT 2018 study were not fetched.
