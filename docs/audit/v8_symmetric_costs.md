## 5. FER-X December 2025

- PV 56.82 average (max 62.675); wind 72.85 (max 77.738); 20-year two-way CfD [20] [25].
- Not a clean cost: price is updated with the ISTAT consumer price index [23]; +10 EUR/MWh North and +4 Centre are added on top [22] [23]; payment on producible energy when curtailed and a zero floor on the reference price [22]; cumulation allowed with capital grants up to 40% (price cut up to 35%), guarantee funds and tax credits for machinery investment [22] [23]. Whether winners actually hold ZES Unica or similar credits was not found. Guarantees of origin: not found.
- About 70% of capacity in Sicily and Puglia; three quarters of projects under 10 MW [20] [25].
- Analysts: 56.8 sits at the low end of the 55-75 LCOE range (6% WACC, 25 y), "narrow" margin; wind offers often did not cover costs (940 MW of 1.66 GW admitted) [24]. PPA fair value 58.7 solar, 72.98 wind [21]. Advisers: up to EUR 8 bn capex for 8.64 GW = ~0.93 M EUR/MW (derived) [20].

## 7. Nuclear symmetry

- Dukovany 5-6: up to 976 MWe each, up to 15.4 TWh/yr = 90.0% (derived); 9-year build in the model; 60-year life [28].
- World 2024: capacity factor 83% (82% in 2023); median build of 2024 connections 103 months [29]. IAEA: median load factor 86.4%, top quartile 93.7%, PWR ten-year median 83.7% [30].
- Symmetric choice: 90% matches the official new-build assumption; 83-86% is the observed fleet.

## 9. Grid integration

- Terna PdS 2025: over EUR 23 bn 2025-2034, up to ~40 bn in total; zonal transfer 16 to over 35 GW; +65 GW RES by 2030, of which ~43 GW on the HV grid [38]. Derived: 23 bn / 65 GW = ~350 EUR/kW (upper bound; plan also serves security and demand).
- e-distribuzione 2025-2029: ~EUR 12.0 bn (HV 1.9, MV 5.9, LV 3.4, digital 0.8), no split for distributed generation; 63 GW of producers connected by 2027, over 18 GW new [39].
- Connection paid by plant: 70 EUR/kWp (1 MW) [3]; 8-14% of PV LCOE, 4-6% of wind LCOE [1]. Requests to Terna end 2024: PV 152 GW, onshore wind 110 GW, stand-alone storage 253 GW [41].

## Where sources disagree

1. BESS: ARERA 2025 (209.5) vs ARERA 2026 (150.5) vs auction-implied ~100 EUR/kWh. For 2024-25 contracts use ~170 with oversizing; ~100 is an investment-volume headline from Enel-scale procurement.
2. PV life and degradation: Fraunhofer (30 y, 0.25%) is an assumption; observed fleet data (0.75%) and Italian official costings (20-25 y) are more conservative and more reliable.
3. PV yield: developer P50 (1,600-2,100) vs observed fleet (1,066-1,117). The fleet is mostly old rooftop, so it understates new trackers; P50 overstates delivered energy.
4. Wind hours: 2,000-2,500 assumed vs 1,626 observed fleet in 2025.
5. Electrolyser: IEA 2,000-2,600 USD/kW installed vs much lower vendor and Chinese figures; use IEA.
6. Nuclear: 90% (project model) vs 83% (world fleet).
